# Trade-off reporting

## Procedure

1. List the two or three best candidates with value, final error, fuel factor and scrap proxy.
2. State what improves and what worsens between them, in plain terms.
3. Say which one you would pick for the stated objective and why.
4. Note anything the twin cannot show: orders at risk, spares, equipment condition.

## Rules

- Candidates are forecasts. Recovery must still be measured on fresh samples.
- You do not issue commands. The Gateway validates anything the main harness proposes.
